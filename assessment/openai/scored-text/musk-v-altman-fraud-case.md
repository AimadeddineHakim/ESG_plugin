A federal judge scheduled the Musk v. Altman fraud case for trial in April 2026. The lawsuit centers on OpenAI's alleged betrayal of its founding nonprofit mission. Co-founder Greg Brockman wrote "it was a lie" in his private diary in November 2017, regarding the organization's nonprofit commitment.

Timeline: 2015 — Musk donates $38M to OpenAI as a nonprofit; 2017 — Brockman's diary entry questions the nonprofit commitment; 2019 — OpenAI creates a for-profit subsidiary and takes $1B (subsequently ~$13B) from Microsoft; 2025 — that subsidiary reaches a ~$500 billion valuation; the nonprofit now controls less than a third of the company. Former Chief Scientist Ilya Sutskever questioned Sam Altman's motivations: "We don't understand your cost function. Is AGI truly your primary motivation?"

=== Follow-up research (gap-fill, 2026-09-21) ===
Question: What is the current status of the Musk v. Altman (OpenAI) fraud/breach-of-mission lawsuit as of September 2026 — has a trial occurred, what was the outcome, and is the case ongoing, settled, dismissed, concluded, or under appeal?

Finding: The case went to a jury trial in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California (Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers), with testimony from Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Satya Nadella, and Musk himself. On May 18, 2026, the jury ruled against Musk, dismissing all claims — including the breach-of-charitable-trust/nonprofit-mission claims — on statute-of-limitations grounds (filed outside the applicable three-year window), not on the merits. Musk called the verdict a "calendar technicality" and vowed to appeal. A notice of appeal was subsequently filed and the case is now docketed at the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals ("X.AI Corp., et al. v. OpenAI, Inc., et al.," No. 26-4486); the appellate process is expected to extend into 2027. Sources: CNBC, NPR, PBS NewsHour, NBC News, Al Jazeera, Wikipedia ("Musk v. Altman"), Justia Ninth Circuit docket No. 26-4486 (accessed 2026-09-21).

Overall: trial-court proceedings concluded with a verdict for OpenAI/Altman; the case as a whole remains under appeal, not yet fully and finally concluded.
