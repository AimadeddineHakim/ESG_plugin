# doj-worker-discrimination-settlement

## Source 1: Civil Rights Division Secures Settlement with OpenAI for Discriminating Against U.S. Workers
URL: https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/civil-rights-division-secures-settlement-openai-discriminating-against-us-workers
(body used: sources/doj-worker-discrimination-settlement.md)

The Justice Department's Civil Rights Division announced (August 4, 2026) a combined $3,200,000 settlement with OpenAI OpCo LLC and its subsidiary Statsig Inc. addressing allegations that both companies violated the Immigration and Nationality Act by discriminating against U.S. workers in favor of temporary visa holders during Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) hiring.

The Department's investigation found OpenAI did not advertise PERM positions on its external job website (unlike other jobs), required paper-only applications for PERM positions while allowing electronic applications elsewhere, and advertised positions on late-night radio to discourage U.S. worker applications. Fewer than ten PERM positions were at issue.

Under the settlement: OpenAI will pay $1,200,000 in civil penalties and establish a $2,000,000 back-pay fund for affected workers; post PERM positions publicly and accept electronic applications; train personnel on INA anti-discrimination requirements; revise employment policies; and remain subject to departmental monitoring and reporting requirements.

This is the thirteenth settlement under the Department's Protecting U.S. Workers Initiative (relaunched 2025). Penalties have been agreed and remediation steps specified, though ongoing DOJ monitoring/reporting requirements remain in effect.
