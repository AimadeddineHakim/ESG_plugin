# navier-stokes-dispute

## Source 1: A string of controversies hits OpenAI, Anthropic (Fortune)
URL: https://fortune.com/2026/09/10/a-string-of-controversies-hits-openai-anthropic/
(body used: sources/controversies-string-hits-openai-anthropic.md)

Over the weekend, social media buzzed about OpenAI solving the difficult Navier-Stokes math problem for the first time in history. Two mathematicians who were working on the problem using Codex accused OpenAI of combing through their user logs to steal their work and solve the problem first. OpenAI denies this but admits it doesn't know for certain whether the models trained on the mathematicians' work: "While unlikely, we cannot rule out that de-identified data derived from their usage of our products helped improve our models," OpenAI wrote in a post.

The dispute involves two named individuals (the mathematicians) and is explicitly contested — OpenAI denies intentional misuse but cannot fully rule out data influence. No settlement, correction, or resolution is described; the allegation remains open/unresolved as of this source.
