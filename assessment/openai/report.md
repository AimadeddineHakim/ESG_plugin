## 1. Tumbler Ridge Mass-Shooting Lawsuits — Red Flag (Score: 0, Severity: Very Severe)

**Category / Pillar:** Social — Customers : Product Safety & Quality
**Nature of Harm:** Very Serious · **Scale of Impact:** Extensive · **Company Role:** Direct · **Case Status:** Active / Ongoing

New lawsuits filed September 2, 2026 accuse OpenAI executives of prioritizing public image over public safety in connection with the February 2026 Tumbler Ridge, British Columbia school shooting, which killed six people and injured roughly two dozen others. Plaintiffs allege ChatGPT was used to plan the attack and that OpenAI's own systems flagged credible threat signals eight months earlier, with a recommendation to alert police allegedly overridden by the company's global-affairs/PR leadership. Thirty new plaintiffs joined seven existing plaintiff households; OpenAI disputes the internal-decision-making allegations and is separately seeking dismissal of the earlier suits on venue grounds. OpenAI/ChatGPT is named in the majority of 40+ similar chatbot-harm lawsuits nationally.

**Sources:** https://www.npr.org/2026/09/02/nx-s1-5953021/openai-tumbler-ridge-mass-shooting

## 2. Effingham County Data Center Outrage — Yellow Flag (Score: 2, Severity: Severe)

**Category / Pillar:** Social — Human Rights & Community Impact : Impact on Communities
**Nature of Harm:** Medium · **Scale of Impact:** Extremely Widespread · **Company Role:** Direct · **Case Status:** Active / Partially Concluded

OpenAI's $20 billion, 2,600-acre data center project in Effingham County, Georgia, was negotiated in secret for months and announced without public comment or a county vote. Nearly 1,000 residents attended a contentious meeting citing noise/light pollution, rising electricity costs, and a lack of democratic input; a cancellation petition drew over 7,000 signatures. OpenAI has committed $80 million in community benefits and at least 400 jobs, and the deal is finalized, but opposition and moratorium activity in neighboring counties continue.

**Sources:** https://thecurrentga.org/2026/07/23/openai-data-center-project-sparks-outrage-among-effingham-county-residents/

## 3. Musk v. Altman Fraud Case — Yellow Flag (Score: 2, Severity: Severe)

**Category / Pillar:** Governance — Governance Structures
**Nature of Harm:** Serious · **Scale of Impact:** Extensive · **Company Role:** Direct · **Case Status:** Active / Partially Concluded

Elon Musk's lawsuit alleges OpenAI abandoned its founding nonprofit mission after his ~$38M in donations, converting to a for-profit structure that took ~$13B from Microsoft and reached a ~$500B valuation, with the nonprofit retaining less than a third of control. The case went to jury trial in spring 2026; on May 18, 2026 the jury ruled for OpenAI/Altman on statute-of-limitations grounds (not on the merits). Musk has since filed a notice of appeal, now docketed at the Ninth Circuit (No. 26-4486), so the case remains active on appeal despite the trial-court resolution.

**Sources:** https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/the-open-ai-fraud-case-reveals-a-broken-system-here-s-the-fix-nobody-s-talking-about

## 4. ChatGPT's Environmental Footprint — Green Flag (Score: 6, Severity: Minor)

**Category / Pillar:** Environmental — Energy & Climate Change
**Nature of Harm:** Minimal · **Scale of Impact:** Limited · **Company Role:** Direct · **Case Status:** Active / Ongoing

As ChatGPT reached 700 million weekly users, its aggregate electricity, carbon, and water footprint scaled accordingly: an estimated 260,000+ kg of CO2 per month, 340+ MWh of daily inference electricity (comparable to 30,000 U.S. homes), and a water draw of roughly half a liter per 20-50 queries. This is a diffuse, ongoing byproduct of scaling operations rather than a bounded environmental damage event.
