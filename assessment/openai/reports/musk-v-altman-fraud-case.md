# The OpenAI Fraud Case Reveals a Broken System. Here's the Fix Nobody's Talking About.

## Article Summary

**The Core Narrative:**
A federal judge has scheduled the Musk v. Altman fraud case for trial in April 2026. The lawsuit centers on OpenAI's alleged betrayal of its nonprofit mission. According to the article, co-founder Greg Brockman wrote "it was a lie" in his private diary regarding the organization's nonprofit commitment in November 2017.

**Timeline of Events:**
- 2015: Elon Musk donated $38 million to OpenAI as a nonprofit venture
- 2017: Internal diary entries questioned the nonprofit commitment
- 2019: OpenAI established a for-profit subsidiary and secured $1 billion from Microsoft
- 2025: That subsidiary reached a $500 billion valuation
- Present: The nonprofit controls less than one-third of the company

**Key Allegation:**
Former Chief Scientist Ilya Sutskever questioned Sam Altman's motivations, asking "We don't understand your cost function. Is AGI truly your primary motivation?"

**Proposed Solution:**
The author advocates for Wyoming's DUNA (Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association) framework, which combines nonprofit status with token governance. They created WYDE, claiming it achieved the first 501(c)(4) nonprofit status for a DUNA structure, funding charitable work through trading fees rather than traditional donations.
