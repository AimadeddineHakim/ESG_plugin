# Civil Rights Division Secures Settlement with OpenAI for Discriminating Against U.S. Workers

**Date:** Tuesday, August 4, 2026

The Justice Department's Civil Rights Division announced a $3.2 million settlement with OpenAI OpCo LLC and its subsidiary Statsig Inc. for violating the Immigration and Nationality Act by discriminating against U.S. workers in favor of those holding temporary employment visas during the Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) hiring process.

**Investigative Findings:**

The department discovered that OpenAI did not advertise PERM positions on its external job website despite doing so for other roles. The company also required paper applications for these positions while accepting electronic submissions elsewhere. Additionally, OpenAI advertised positions on late-night radio to discourage U.S. worker applications.

**Settlement Terms:**

- $1.2 million in civil penalties
- $2 million back-pay fund for affected workers
- Requirement to post PERM positions on public career website
- Acceptance of electronic applications for PERM roles
- Mandatory INA anti-discrimination training for personnel
- Policy revisions and departmental monitoring

Assistant Attorney General Harmeet K. Dhillon stated: "It is illegal to discriminate against U.S. workers by preferring temporary visa holders for jobs."

This represents the thirteenth settlement under the department's Protecting U.S. Workers Initiative, launched in 2025 to enforce citizenship status discrimination prohibitions.
