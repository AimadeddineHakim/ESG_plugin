ChatGPT has reached 700 million weekly active users, quadrupling its user base in under a year. This explosive growth raises significant environmental concerns across three key areas.

Carbon Emissions: Each ChatGPT query consumes about 0.3 to 0.4 watt-hours of electricity, resulting in roughly 0.15 grams of CO2 per response. At scale, the platform may generate over 260,000 kilograms of monthly CO2 -- equivalent to 260 transatlantic round-trip flights.

Energy Consumption: Supporting 700 million weekly users requires approximately 340 megawatt-hours daily for inference alone -- comparable to electricity usage across 30,000 American homes. Training GPT-3 consumed 1,287 MWh and produced 550 metric tons of CO2.

Water Usage: Every 20 to 50 queries to ChatGPT uses about half a liter of water for cooling systems. The broader AI industry is projected to consume 4.2-6.6 billion cubic meters annually by 2027 -- matching household water consumption for millions of people.

Environmental Factors: Complex prompts consume up to 50 times more energy than simple queries. Data center location, power sources, and cooling methods significantly influence overall environmental impact. Industry leaders are increasingly adopting renewable energy and efficiency improvements to address these concerns.
