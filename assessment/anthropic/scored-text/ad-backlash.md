# Controversy: ad-backlash

Company: Anthropic

=== The Backlash to Anthropic's Ad Misses Something Bigger (https://time.com/article/2026/07/16/anthropic-ai-marketing-advertisement/) ===

Anthropic's recent advertising campaign faced significant online criticism. The video features audio of people raising concerns about AI—"Can AI be trusted?" and "Who's going to hit the brakes?"—paired with images of protests, homelessness, and fire. The ad then shifts tone, asking "Could AI help people stop feeling misunderstood?" and concludes with "There's hope in hard questions" followed by Anthropic's tagline "Keep thinking."

The response was largely negative. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman suggested it seemed like satire, while a top YouTube comment called it "dystopian marketing slop." Atlantic writer Lila Shroff characterized the campaign as "tone-deaf." Critics argued Anthropic appeared to "scare everyone about AI, then pose as the only ones humble enough to be trusted."

This represents Anthropic's second divisive commercial. Their Super Bowl ad mocking OpenAI won a Cannes Lions award but ranked in the bottom 3% for audience likeability, partly because most viewers didn't understand the reference.

Anthropic explained the campaign was based on interviews with over 120,000 people across 159 countries conducted during a U.S. roadshow. Company president Daniela Amodei stated the goal was demonstrating that Anthropic, as an AI model developer, genuinely wanted to hear public concerns.

The controversy reflects deeper skepticism: many dismiss AI industry safety warnings as marketing. However, the author argues candor about risks—even if self-serving—surpasses deception. CEO Dario Amodei has estimated AI poses a 10-25% chance of catastrophic outcomes, making stark imagery arguably more honest than previous campaigns presenting Claude as merely a problem-solving tool.
