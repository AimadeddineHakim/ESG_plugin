# Anthropic Safety Researcher Resigns with AI Warning

Jacob Coxon, a researcher at Anthropic, resigned on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, and issued a warning to colleagues via Slack.

According to the NBC News report from September 9, 2026, Coxon communicated concerns about superintelligent AI development. He warned that without increased caution and collaborative efforts in the industry, advanced AI systems pose "a risk of causing human extinction."

Coxon told NBC News that "the AI industry is set up for potentially catastrophic failure."

Additional reporting from other outlets (Time, Axios, Scientific American) indicates Coxon spent roughly three years doing pretraining research at both OpenAI and Anthropic, gave up his equity to leave the company, and said companies are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence and gambling with our lives." He called for a temporary ban on capability improvements to allow international coordination and safety oversight to catch up. His public statement was viewed over 155 million times and prompted several other AI researchers to speak out.
