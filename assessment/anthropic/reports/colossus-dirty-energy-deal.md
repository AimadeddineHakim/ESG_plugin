# Anthropic's Fossil Fuel Data Center Deal Criticized

Climate analyst Ketan Joshi argues that Anthropic's partnership with xAI's Colossus data center in Memphis—a facility notorious for pollution—contradicts the company's green-conscious image.

## The Core Issue

Anthropic secured computing power from one of America's most polluting facilities after the #QuitGPT campaign encouraged users to switch from OpenAI's ChatGPT to Claude. This irony underscores what Joshi views as performative environmentalism masking harmful business practices.

## Environmental Concerns

The Colossus facility generates massive emissions. Joshi estimates that xAI's entire data center fleet "will likely undo the entire emissions saved by Tesla's global fleet of electric vehicles." Recently, xAI added 500 megawatts of unpermitted gas turbines to Colossus 2, increasing its climate footprint by 65%.

The accompanying 30-megawatt solar installation reduces emissions by only "1.8% of consumption," with plans shrinking to "0.3% of total" once other stages launch. Powering the site entirely through solar would require approximately "10,000 acres" of panels—roughly 115 times the current installation.

## Broader Pattern

Joshi contends that Colossus represents industry norms rather than exceptions. His database identifies 184 planned or operational data centers with larger environmental footprints, demonstrating systemic prioritization of "corrosive and ugly development."
