# Anthropic–United States Department of Defense Dispute: Overview

## Core Conflict

Since January 2026, Anthropic and the U.S. Department of Defense have clashed over military AI usage. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth publicly criticized Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's approach to artificial intelligence, particularly regarding restrictions on lethal weapons and surveillance applications.

## Key Timeline

**Background tensions (2025):**
- Amodei criticized the Trump administration's Stargate AI project as "chaotic"
- Anthropic opposed rescission of Biden's Executive Order on Artificial Intelligence
- The company hired several Biden administration officials, drawing Trump administration criticism
- Amodei skipped Trump's inauguration to attend the World Economic Forum

**Escalation (January-February 2026):**
- DoD announced GenAI.mil platform, initially using Google Gemini, then OpenAI's ChatGPT
- Hegseth promoted xAI's Grok, denouncing "woke AI" (widely understood as referencing Anthropic)
- Pentagon and Anthropic conflicted over use restrictions—a "$200 million" contract termination resulted
- DoD threatened to use Anthropic's Claude for "all lawful purposes"; the company refused

**Government retaliation:**
- Hegseth moved to designate Anthropic a "supply chain risk," forcing military contractors to sever ties
- Emil Michael, Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering, urged Anthropic to "cross the Rubicon"

## Legal Proceedings

**Northern District of California (March 2026):**
Judge Rita F. Lin granted preliminary injunction, finding the designation constituted First Amendment retaliation. She noted: "Punishing Anthropic for bringing public scrutiny to the government's contracting position is classic illegal retaliation."

**D.C. Circuit Court (April 2026):**
The appeals court denied Anthropic's emergency motion to lift the FASCSA designation, citing ongoing military conflict concerns.

## Central Issues

Anthropic maintained ethical guardrails prohibiting Claude's use for:
- Lethal autonomous weapons development
- Mass surveillance operations
- Undisclosed military applications

The Pentagon demanded unrestricted access; Anthropic refused, prioritizing safety protocols over government demands.

## Broader Consequences

- Trump-aligned venture capital firm 1789 Capital abandoned a planned "hundreds of millions" investment
- OpenAI rapidly negotiated Pentagon access without similar restrictions
- Claude reportedly used in February 2026 Venezuela intervention and alleged March 2026 Iranian school strike (156 killed, including 120 children, per Amnesty International)

The dispute revealed tensions between national security interests and corporate governance of powerful AI systems.
