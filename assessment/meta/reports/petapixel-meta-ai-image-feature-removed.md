Meta discontinued an AI image generation feature ("Muse Image," launched July 7, 2026, within Meta AI chat on Instagram) after facing significant backlash from users, creators, and organizations over its default opt-in settings.

The feature: Meta expanded its AI image generation capabilities to allow users to create new images based on public Instagram posts. Users could @-mention a public Instagram account to have Meta AI reference that account's photos as visual inspiration for generated images.

The problem: the feature was enabled by default for all public Instagram accounts. Users had to manually disable the setting if they didn't want their publicly shared images used this way. Critics argued that many Instagram users were unaware their content could be referenced without explicit permission, and creators raised concerns about others imitating their artistic style without consent.

Opposition: Public Citizen called the feature an "egregious invasion" of people's privacy. The talent agency CAA called for Meta to require affirmative consent before someone's likeness could be used. SAG-AFTRA, the largest labor union for actors, recommended its members turn off sharing and urged all Instagram users to opt out of the Muse Image tool.

Meta's response: following three days of pressure, Meta disabled the @-mention image generation feature entirely on July 10, 2026, stating: "We've heard feedback that people found this confusing, so we've removed this feature while we take time to listen to feedback and evaluate the approach." Meta has not announced plans to reintroduce the feature or provide an alternative, consent-first system.
