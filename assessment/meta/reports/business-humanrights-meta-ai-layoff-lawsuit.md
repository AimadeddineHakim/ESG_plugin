Twenty-six Meta employees filed a lawsuit in July 2026 claiming the company deployed artificial intelligence systems to identify workers for termination. The plaintiffs contend that these AI tools systematically disadvantaged staff members who had taken maternity leave, medical leave, or other legally protected time off, as well as those requiring disability accommodations.

The complaint states that Meta's AI systems evaluated worker performance using metrics that weren't available during protected absences or were diminished for employees with disabilities. This created a scenario where "employees who took protected leaves were disproportionately selected for layoff, based on scoring that not only failed to account for their protected leaves, but in effect penalized the employees for exercising their legal rights to these leaves."

The affected workers are seeking preliminary injunctive relief to halt the layoffs pending litigation, plus potential reinstatement, back compensation, lost equity, and additional damages.

Meta disputed the allegations through a spokesperson, stating: "Workforce management and organizational decisions were and are made by people, not AI."
